# Kingbird brand knowledge base

Reviewed October 29, 2026. Sources are brand-owned materials, rather than the previous prototype's placeholder copy.

## Positioning and voice

Kingbird is a dry, tropical, wine-based American aperitif. The strongest occasion is the dinner party: an unexpected alternative to bringing wine, simple to serve, and made for sharing. Voice: short, social, confident, warm, slightly irreverent. Sell the occasion before the technical category. Avoid wellness promises, invented awards or reviews, artificial scarcity, and generic luxury language.

## Visual identity

Use the official Kingbird wordmark and bird emblem, rather than rebuilding the logo in a font. Obviously Variable provides bold, compressed display typography; Della Respira provides the contrasting editorial serif used by the live site. Cream/ink label colors, red/orange liquid, and warm party photography form the working campaign palette. The six-page KINGBIRD-BRAND-WK.pdf (August 2023 file) was downloaded, rendered and visually reviewed. Its off-white label studies, black/cream bird identity, red bottle/label explorations and oversized poster typography support the chosen direction. It is a label-and-posters deck, with no specified font or hexadecimal color tokens. It also uses an older “American Dry Punch” product name; newer product assets and the current storefront take precedence. The working palette is derived from packaging and photography, rather than claimed as exact style-guide tokens. Full-bleed social photography alternates with restrained product compositions. Avoid stock or generated visuals.

## Product facts and decisions

- Wine-based American aperitif, produced in Accord, New York: supported by supplied sources.
- Finger Lakes white wine and the named fruits/botanicals: supported by the info sheet.
- Sensory description: dry, tropical, citrus-forward and delicately bitter. No quantified sugar, calorie, health or comparative sweetness claims.
- 750 mL per bottle: current Shopify title. Two-bottle package is $72, verified on review date. Link to the live product page for authoritative availability and final pricing.
- ABV conflict: info sheet says label 20%, laboratory result closer to 18%; live product title still says 20%. Do not publish an 18% claim or calculate alcohol equivalences. Replace the preliminary lower-alcohol story with a verified bubbles-and-ice serving occasion.
- Shipping conflict: older info sheet lists NY, CA, DC, FL; investor memo says 42 states; current PDP says nationwide. Do not turn that into an unconditional nationwide promise. Shipping eligibility, delivery and taxes are resolved by the merchant store.
- Hospitality references in investor memo are historical and require current confirmation. Story eight uses the verified founder/production story instead of venue logos, testimonials or ratings.
- Carmine: product is not vegan. Ingredient information includes this rather than making an all-natural or chemical-free claim.

2. Authoritative shipping restrictions and delivery terms; the landing page age attestation is not identity verification.
3. Niitrops screenshots/video are referenced but absent from attachments. Interaction follows the written specification.

Other purchase links retain the product-page option. The cart handoff was tested: exactly one two-bottle variant, $72 subtotal; the test item was removed afterward. 7. Full historical media-library audit and exact campaign palette approval.
